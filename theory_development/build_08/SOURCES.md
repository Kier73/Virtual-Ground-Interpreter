# Build 08 — Sources

1. Kriegeskorte et al., *Representational Similarity Analysis — Connecting the Branches of Systems Neuroscience*. Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1093/scan/nsz099
   - Methodological grounding for constructing and comparing representational dissimilarity matrices.

2. Diedrichsen et al., *Statistical inference on representational geometries*. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10446828/
   - Inference across subjects and conditions; emphasizes bootstrap and cross-validation design for representational geometries.

3. Chazal et al., *The Structure and Stability of Persistence Modules*. https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~arinaldo/papers/BandforRips.pdf
   - Persistence modules and stability ideas relevant to Vietoris–Rips filtrations.

4. Hensel et al., *A Survey of Topological Machine Learning Methods*. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8187791/
   - Overview of topological machine learning and persistence stability.

5. Edelsbrunner & Harer, *Persistent Homology*. https://vis.lbl.gov/PDFs/PH-handbook.pdf
   - General reference for persistence diagrams, bottleneck distances, and persistent homology.

These sources support the methods and theoretical background. They do not validate the synthetic Virtual Layer conjectures or the numerical results generated in this build.
