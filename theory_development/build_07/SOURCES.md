# Build 07 — Research sources

These sources support the established methods used in the experiment. The specific codonic path library, observer definitions and numerical results are original synthetic test constructions for this build.

1. **Kriegeskorte, N., Mur, M. & Bandettini, P. (2008).** Representational similarity analysis — connecting the branches of systems neuroscience. *Frontiers in Systems Neuroscience*, 2:4. https://doi.org/10.3389/neuro.06.004.2008
   - Foundation for comparing representational dissimilarity matrices without requiring identical raw coordinate systems.

2. **Mattioni, S. (2024).** Exploring brain representations through the lens of similarity structures. *Nature Reviews Psychology*, 3, 506–520. https://doi.org/10.1038/s44159-024-00335-8
   - Contemporary overview of similarity-structure approaches and RDMs.

3. **Chazal, F., Glisse, M., Labruère, C. & Michel, B. (2015).** Convergence Rates for Persistence Diagram Estimation in Topological Data Analysis. *Journal of Machine Learning Research*, 16(110), 3603–3635. https://jmlr.org/papers/v16/chazal15a.html
   - Statistical treatment of persistent homology in metric spaces and convergence of persistence diagrams.

4. **Fernández, X., Borghini, E., Mindlin, G. & Groisman, P. (2023).** Intrinsic Persistent Homology via Density-based Metric Learning. *Journal of Machine Learning Research*, 24(75), 1–42. https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume24/21-1044/21-1044.pdf
   - Metric choice, intrinsic geometry and robustness in persistent homology.

5. **Kriegeskorte, N. & Wei, X.-X. (2021).** Neural tuning and representational geometry. *Nature Reviews Neuroscience*, 22, 703–718. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41583-021-00502-3
   - Relates sensitivity and tuning to representational geometry.

6. **Chung, F. (1997).** *Spectral Graph Theory*. CBMS Regional Conference Series in Mathematics, 92. American Mathematical Society. https://bookstore.ams.org/cbms-92
   - Mathematical basis for graph Laplacians and their spectra.

## Source/result boundary

The cited literature establishes methods such as RDM comparison, persistent homology and graph spectral analysis. It does not establish the Build 07 numerical findings, a universal perceptual geometry, or the proposed interpretation of codonic tone. Those remain experimental observations and conjectures specific to this prototype.
