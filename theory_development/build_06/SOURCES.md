# Build 06 — Research sources

## Hopf fibration and quaternion geometry

**David W. Lyons, “An Elementary Introduction to the Hopf Fibration.”**

A clear quaternion-based exposition of the Hopf map `S³ → S²`, quaternionic rotations, unit quaternions, and the double-cover relationship with three-dimensional rotations.

- [Paper PDF](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=1afe19525893dc5f34ac87481a16bf220c7c7bd5&repid=rep1&type=pdf)
- Relevant definitions: Hopf map and unit sphere, pp. 2–5; quaternion representation of rotations and the `q` / `-q` equivalence, pp. 5–7.

## Adaptive sensing and measurement cost

**Michele Kelley and Robert McMichael (2025), “Weighing unequal parameter importance and measurement expense in adaptive quantum sensing,” Journal of Applied Physics 137(7).**

Describes Bayesian adaptive experiment design that chooses where to measure based on information gain, parameter importance, and measurement expense. The Build 06 selector uses a small synthetic EIG-per-cost objective; it is not a replication of their sensing system.

- [NIST publication page](https://www.nist.gov/publications/weighing-unequal-parameter-importance-and-measurement-expense-adaptive-quantum-sensing)
- [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0251881)

## Observable equivalence and quotient state representations

**Yan Jiao, Pin-Han Ho, and Limei Peng (published October 9, 2026), “Observable quotient world models for knowledge-state abstraction under partial observability,” Knowledge-Based Systems 351, article 116768.**

Introduces action-conditioned observation-law equivalence classes, quotient beliefs, and quotient information gain for sensing-action selection. This is closely related in motivation to the finite observer-specific partitions in Build 06. The present prototype is much smaller and does not implement their formal theory or learned world model.

- [University research record and abstract](https://research.knu.ac.kr/en/publications/observable-quotient-world-models-for-knowledge-state-abstraction-/)
- [Publisher record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0950705126014942)
- [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2026.116768)

## Scope note

These sources establish the mathematical and methodological foundations cited in this build. They do not establish the proposed Virtual Layer interpretation of codonic tone, universal meaning spaces, or a biological equivalence between emotional perception and quaternionic holonomy.
