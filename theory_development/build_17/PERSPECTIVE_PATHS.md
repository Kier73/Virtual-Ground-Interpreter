## A compact selection rule

1. Specify the object: signal, event stream, graph, path, group action, operator, or parameter space.
2. Specify the equivalence: translation, scale, rotation, relabeling, reparameterization, basis change, or functional equivalence.
3. Specify the observer and the measured output.
4. Choose a representation that intertwines the action or makes its orbits easy to compare.
5. State which information is preserved, discarded, or only preserved approximately.
6. Test a transformation inside the assumed symmetry and a negative control outside it.

## Invariant hierarchy

- **Exact algebraic invariant:** follows from a proved identity or representation property.
- **Exact finite-fixture invariant:** follows from the defined discrete boundary conditions and implementation.
- **Statistical invariant:** holds within a specified noise, sampling and calibration model.
- **Approximate similarity:** distances remain small under a stated tolerance.
- **Convenient coordinate:** simplifies calculation but is not itself invariant.

Do not use “invariant” without naming the group/action, observer, measure, and boundary convention.
