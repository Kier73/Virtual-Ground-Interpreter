# Build 21 Experiment Protocol

## Fixture

A two-reservoir discrete canal model with capacity 3 per reservoir and four environmental phases. Phase schedules:

- inflow to reservoir A: `(1,0,0,1)`;
- demand from reservoir B: `(0,1,0,0)`;
- phase progression: `(1,2,3,0)`.

Legal controller actions: HOLD, A_TO_B, B_TO_A, subject to source inventory and destination capacity.

## Required invariants

1. Every state respects capacity and phase bounds.
2. Gate transfer conserves total inventory before environmental forcing.
3. Each transition obeys exact ledger mass balance.
4. Environmental phase progression follows the declared phase map.
5. Observer partitions do not claim a memoryless quotient unless action-labelled transition congruence holds.
6. Belief policies choose only actions legal for all possible hidden states.
7. Every policy branch reaches the intention within the declared horizon.
8. Pareto routes are nondominated under the declared cost vector.
9. Viability policies keep every state in the safe kernel.
10. Migration reports distinguish direct identity migration from lossy normalization.

## Reproduction

```bash
python experiment.py
python -m unittest discover -s tests -v
```

No network access or third-party dependencies required.

## Provenance

All Build 21 flow/canal numbers are generated by a deterministic synthetic finite model. They are not physical hydrology measurements and do not claim realistic water-system dynamics.
