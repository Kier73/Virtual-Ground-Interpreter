## Mathematical basis used in this build

The signal-coded arithmetic uses the standard finite cyclic group representation:

\[
\chi(k)=e^{2\pi i k/8},\qquad \chi(a+b)=\chi(a)\chi(b)
\]

This identity is proved directly by the exponent law and exhaustively tested over all operand pairs in the finite domain.

No external empirical dataset or web source was used for Build 30 results. All fixtures and numeric result tables are generated locally by the included scripts.
