# Build Lineage — 20 through 25

- **Build 20:** finite generative Virtual Grounds, reversible and partial transition systems, intention-relative navigation.
- **Build 21:** endogenous dynamics, controlled variables, environment, belief navigation, resources and viability.
- **Build 22:** active identification within a supplied candidate family and held-out open-world validation.
- **Build 23:** symbolic rule induction, counterexample-driven grammar expansion and executable ground serialization.
- **Build 24:** resettable latent predictive-context discovery and compilation to a phase program or finite automaton.
- **Build 25:** non-resettable online tracking of unknown predictive context, asynchronous partial observations, local-clock discipline, safe probe suggestions, contradiction quarantine and bounded memory.

## Important boundary

Build 25 consumes a Build 24 predictive ground. It does not yet infer arbitrary transition laws online from a continuing stream. Build 26 should combine online context tracking with incremental structural induction and explicit model revision.
